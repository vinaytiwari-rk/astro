# Jyotish Hora

Modern Vedic Astrology & Ephemeris Studio inspired by Jagannatha Hora, built with React, TypeScript, Vite, and `astronomy-engine`.

## Features

- **Birth Charts & Styles:** North Indian (Diamond), South Indian (Square), and East Indian (Bengali/Oriya) SVG charts with downloadable SVG export and print-ready PDF layout.
- **Modular 15-Pillar Astrology Prediction Studio (`Predictions`):** Organized into: **1. Events, Accidents, Paya, Gandmool & Complete Life Q&A**, **2. Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN — Karaka Chains, 1-5-9 Directional Trines, Vakri Backward Rules, Nadi Disease Alerts & 12-Yr Guru Cycles)**, **3. Complete 144 House-Lord-in-House Matrix (`12 × 12` Chart Placements & Explorer)**, **4. Yuti-Wise Conjunctions (`2` to `7` Planets)**, **5. Bhavat Bhavam Wise (`H → 2H − 1` Mirror Houses + Interactive Relational Calculator)**, **6. Aspects / Drishti Wise (`Graha Drishti`, Parashari Virupas, Mutual Oppositions, 12-House Aspect Matrix & Drishti Explorer)**, **7. Planets in Signs** (+ 12-Sign Explorer), **8. Planets in Nakshatras & Padas**, **9. Planets in Houses**, **10. House-wise Synthesis (H1–H12)**, **11. Degree-wise (0°–30° Ansha)**, **12. Dasha-wise**, **13. Gochar-wise**, **14. Divisional Charts (D2–D60)**, and **15. Astrology Branches & Numerology**.
- **Classical Vedic Predictions & Life Reading:** Dynamic chart synthesis citing *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra*, *Phaladeepika*, *Saravali*, *Brihat Jataka*, *Uttara Kalamrita*, and *Jaimini Sutras* — plus downloadable `.txt` reports.
- **Specialized Astrology Branches:** Dedicated workspaces with side-by-side Divisional Chart SVGs for Financial Astrology (Indu Lagna, D2/D4), Education (D24), Career (D10), Marriage (D9), **Intimacy & Sexuality (*Kama & Shayana Sukha*, D9/D30)**, Child/Progeny (D7), **Childhood, Parents, Siblings & Family Lineage (D12/D3)**, Life Path & Spirituality (D20), Medical/Ayurvedic (D6, Tridosha, Kalapurusha focus zones), plus Astro-Vastu, Tara Bala Muhurta, and a Western–Vedic Tropical/Sidereal Bridge.
- **Full Kundali Matching & Synastry:** Goes beyond 36-point Ashtakoota Guna Milan with side-by-side Partner D1 & D9 Navamsa charts, D1 Lagna/Moon axis synastry, **Papa-Samyam** malefic balance across Lagna/Moon/Venus, **Sexual & Passion Compatibility** (Venus–Mars cross-chemistry, 12th/8th house overlays, Yoni & drive comparison), Progeny/D7 alignment, and Dasha synchronization.
- **Ephemeris & Settings:** Powered by `astronomy-engine` with selectable Ayanamsa (`Lahiri`, `True Chitra`, `KP`, `Raman`, `Fagan-Bradley`, `Yukteswar`, `Sayana`), Mean vs True Lunar Nodes, House systems (`Whole Sign`, `Sripati`, `Equal`), and optional outer planets.
- **20 Divisional Charts (D1–D60):** Full Shodashavarga + D1–D60 gallery with Vargottama indicators, Panchadha Maitri dignities, Chara Karakas, and Vimshopaka Bala.
- **Dashas & Dasha Predictions:** 4-level Vimshottari Dasha (`Mahadasha` → `Antardasha` → `Pratyantardasha` → `Sookshma`), Yogini Dasha, **Ashtottari (108-Yr)**, **Narayana Dasha (Jaimini)**, **Sudarshana Chakra**, and live **Dasha Phala Predictions** (*BPHS* & *Phaladeepika*) for every clicked period plus full 9-Mahadasha life rating table.
- **Prashna Kundali (Horary Astrology):** Dedicated **Prashna** workspace supporting Live Udaya Lagna or **KP Horary (1–249)**, 10 query categories, Tajika **Ithasala / Easarpha** yogas, Lagna Lord vs Karyesha analysis, Prashna Moon (*Chandra Shuddhi*), Hora omens, timing estimation, and lost-object direction hints.
- **BTR, Avasthas, Muhurta Finder & 10-Year Calendar:** Interactive **Birth Time Rectification** slider (±30m with D9/D10/D60, KP Sub-Lord, **Pranapada** & **Kunda** checks), Planetary **Avasthas** (*Baladi, Jagratadi, Deeptadi*), **Graha Yuddha**, **9 Upagrahas**, **Bhrigu Bindu**, **Beeja & Kshetra Sphuta**, **Muhurta Date-Range Scanner**, and **10-Year Jupiter/Saturn Ingress, Sade Sati & Eclipse Calendar**.
- **Full Bilingual (English & हिन्दी / Hindi) + Multi-Page Brihat Kundli PDF:** Switch between **English and हिन्दी (Hindi)** across all charts, SVG labels, tables, predictions, branches, modals, `.txt` reports, and the multi-page **Brihat Kundli PDF Booklet** (Cover, Panchang, Avakahada/Ghatak, D1/Chalit/D9/D10 SVGs, Shadbala, SAV, 120Y Dasha, 10 Doshas, 8 Domains, Specialized Branches, Varshaphala, Lal Kitab Teva, Gemstones & Rudraksha), plus `.svg` and `.jhora.json` export.
- **Avakahada, Ghatak Chakra & Bhava Chalit (Sripati):** Full classical Avakahada (Paya, Yunja, Varna, Vashya, Yoni, Gana, Nadi, Tatva, 108 Name Syllables, Dasha Balance), Ghatak Chakra triggers, and dedicated **Bhava Chalit (Sripati)** SVG chart with Rasi-vs-Chalit house shift table.
- **Prastarashtakavarga & KP Ruling Planets / Tajika Sahams:** Complete `8×12` **Prastarashtakavarga** contributor matrix, live & chart-time **KP Ruling Planets**, **Nakshatra Nadi**, **Panchadhikari**, and all **16 Tajika Sahams**.
- **Lal Kitab Full System:** Kalapurusha Fixed Natal Chart (`H1=Aries`), cyclic **Lal Kitab Varshaphal Chart**, **Dharmi / Andha / Kana Teva** diagnostics, **Sleeping Planets (*Soye Hue Graha*)**, Rins, and the **35-Year Lal Kitab Dasha Chakra**.
- **Daily Choghadiya, Gowri Panchangam & Annual Hindu Calendar:** Local-sunrise Day/Night **Choghadiya** and **Gowri Panchangam**, plus astronomical annual **Sankranti**, **Ekadashi**, **Purnima/Amavasya**, and major Hindu Vrat & Festival Calendar.
- **Numerology, Lo Shu Grid, 12-Rashi Rashifal, Baby Names & Gemstones:** Chaldean & Pythagorean Numerology, Mulank/Bhagyank/Kua Directions, interactive **3×3 Lo Shu Grid** & 8 Planes, Gochar-powered **12-Rashi Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Yearly Rashifal**, **108 Nakshatra Baby Name Finder**, and personalized **Gemstone & 1–14 Mukhi Rudraksha Advisor**.
- **Mobile-Compatible Responsive UI:** Designed cleanly for phones, tablets, and desktops with sticky mobile bottom navigation, horizontally scrollable subtab ribbons, touch-friendly controls, and zero horizontal viewport overflow.
- **Ashtakavarga:** Bhinna Ashtakavarga, Sarva Ashtakavarga (`SAV = 337`), and Trikona / Ekadhipatya Shodhana.
- **Strength & Yogas:** 6-fold Shadbala breakdown, Panchadha Maitri matrix, Graha Drishti aspects, and classical Yoga/Dosha detection.
- **Transits & Muhurta:** Live or custom-date Gochar overlay (from Moon or Lagna) and daily Muhurta timings (Abhijit, Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika).
- **Match Making:** 36-point Ashtakoota Guna Milan and Manglik Dosha assessment.
- **Offline City Atlas & Profiles:** Built-in city/timezone search (`city-timezones`), local profile persistence, and `.jhora.json` import/export.

```bash
npm run dev      # Start local dev server (http://localhost:5173)
npm test         # Run astronomical & astrological unit tests (Vitest)
npm run build    # Typecheck and build production bundle
npm run preview  # Serve production build locally
```
